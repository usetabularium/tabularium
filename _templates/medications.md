## Legend
- **Kind**: `prescribed` (what a prescriber ordered) · `taken` (what is actually taken: its dose,
  `as needed`, `not taking` or `unknown`).
- One row per dose, each with its own kind, dates and source. A dose change ends its row (To) and
  adds a new one of the same kind; other rows stay. To blank: no end recorded.
- Record *why* in Notes when taken ≠ prescribed — that is the clinically useful part.
