# The Tabularium name

"Tabularium" is the name of this project. It is not a registered trademark, and the licence does not
cover it: the Apache License grants no right to use the name beyond describing where the work came
from and keeping the `NOTICE.md` file (its section 6). This page is a request, not a further licence.
Its aim is safety: anyone who sees the name should find the same safeguards behind it.

## Please feel free to
- write about Tabularium truthfully — describe, teach, review, compare or link to it;
- say where your version came from ("forked from Tabularium") and keep the attribution in
  `NOTICE.md`, as the licence requires;
- use an official release, with your own records and settings added, and call it Tabularium;
- change your own copy for your own use. The rest of this page is about sharing or offering what you
  made — a modified version, or handouts printed from one.

## "Based on Tabularium"
Please give a modified version its own name. Beyond the plain statement of origin above, we ask that
you describe it as "based on Tabularium" only if all three hold:
1. The intended-purpose statement in `NOTICE.md` is kept, unweakened, where its users will see it.
2. These rules from the care contract (`CLAUDE.md`) are kept — reworded or strengthened is fine,
   weakened is not. This list is the whole condition:
   - a possible emergency is answered first, with the emergency number;
   - every fact keeps its source, date and evidence mark, and an original document outranks a
     report, which outranks an inference;
   - the user's own facts are never changed on the AI's inference alone — it asks instead;
   - what was prescribed and what is taken stay separate facts;
   - each person's records stay separate;
   - records leave the user's machine only to the AI model and the backup route the user has chosen
     — never in a web search, a URL or another service;
   - the tool keeps records and prepares questions — the user and their clinicians decide.
3. You say plainly that your version is modified and not endorsed by the Tabularium project.

Handouts say they come from Tabularium. Please keep that line in a modified version only if it meets
all three conditions; otherwise remove it.

## Please don't
- call a modified version "Tabularium", or use the name in the name of a product, service, company
  or domain;
- use the name, "based on" included, for a version that drops or weakens a rule listed in point 2 —
  beyond the statement of origin and the attribution the licence requires;
- suggest an endorsement, certification or affiliation that does not exist.
