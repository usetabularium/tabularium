# ADR-0051 — Human orientation moves off the repository: `site/`, its builder and `OVERVIEW.md` retire

- **Status:** Accepted 2026-09-07
- **Date:** 2026-09-07
- **Deciders:** operator (+ assistant)
- **Migrated under ADR-0061: 2026-11-01** (source revision `12572a0`; the amendment history is in
  git).
- **Supersedes:** ADR-0043 in full, its ring-2 and ring-3 backlog included; ADR-0013 in part
  (point 7, the site © footer carve-out; and in point 3, `OVERVIEW.md` in the export).

## Context

ADR-0043 built a human orientation surface inside the repository: `site/`, four `file://` pages
generated by `tools/site/build_site.py`, committed as tracked HTML and held honest by guard L17. It
answered a real problem, humans onboarding through raw Markdown, while the project had no other
public surface.

It has one now. A public website, in a separate repository that never sees a record, does the same
three jobs better: the walkthrough, the clinician-facing account of the method and the
intended-purpose notice. Keeping `site/` meant two human surfaces kept in agreement by hand, one of
them a page only the maintainer could open.

`OVERVIEW.md`, the clinician one-pager and the site's richest input, is succeeded by the website's
for-clinicians page. The intended-purpose statement keeps the three homes ADR-0035 names:
`README.md`, `NOTICE.md` and the clinician handout's methodology appendix.

## Decision

The operator's instruction of 2026-09-07, during pre-flip maintenance: "delete the /site and the
mention of it in CLAUDE.md … OVERVIEW.md will also be replaced by the website". What holds now:
`checks/publish-skeleton.sh` (`ALLOW` and the identity scan's one carve-out), `checks/README.md`
(*Guards*), `contributing/documentation.md` (*Human orientation*) and `checks/RECORD-SET`.

1. **Delete `site/`, `tools/site/` and `OVERVIEW.md`** (2026-09-07), with the builder's two
   planning documents for profile views (point 4). Their three entries leave `ALLOW`.
2. **Retire guard L17 and selftest §45/§45a; nothing replaces them** (2026-09-07; number not
   reused). L17 proved that a tracked generated artifact was a faithful, in-scope rendering of its
   sources, and none is left to check. **The cost:** the build-QA lanes (zero egress, identifier
   residue, handle tokens, symlinked-source refusal) went too. They kept a tracked page from
   importing `patients/` content or reaching the network. That class is now closed by the
   artifact's absence, not by a check: weaker than a guard, but complete, and the same risk on the
   website sits in a repository that never holds a record.
3. **Withdraw the site © footer identity carve-out** (2026-09-07) and its three selftest bites.
   The top-level `NOTICE.md` carve-out stays, the only content carve-out left since ADR-0032
   retired the render helper's month-array carve-out on 2026-09-29; the path-field anchoring the
   site arm taught (red-team 2026-08-09) governs it.
4. **Rings 2 and 3 are not carried as generated files** (2026-09-07). ADR-0043's parked roster and
   per-person profile views, rendered into the tree, are declined outright. A rendered view of a
   person is a product of the record (three-layer model, `CLAUDE.md`), so it lands under
   `patients/<handle>/`, a `record` path in `checks/RECORD-SET`: gitignored, deny-listed for
   publishing and never tracked. Any list, index or roster of such views is a record too, as
   `patients/README.md` is; an implementation that writes one anywhere else is wrong however
   convenient (2026-09-20). The need stays real. Its proposed form (2026-09-20, an idea only, not
   accepted or scheduled): a skill or custom instruction that produces a preformatted view only
   when the operator asks, with the profile as its source, the view disposable and nothing fed
   back into the record. It avoids silent drift and a permanent build cost for an occasional need,
   and lets the format follow the ask. Before building: if sealed handouts meet every real
   request, build nothing; settle labelling and rendering (roadmap item F5).
5. **Humans are pointed at a URL** (2026-09-07). `README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md` and
   `contributing/documentation.md` name the website (drift recorded 2026-10-11). Every such URL is a
   placeholder until the public flip; the public-release checklist, kept locally, lists what to
   replace or attach before the repository goes public.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep `site/` and let it fall stale** (rejected 2026-09-07): L17 existed because committed
  generated output rots, and a warn-only pilot guard on an unread surface is upkeep with no reader.
- **Keep `OVERVIEW.md` as a clinician one-pager, delete only `site/`** (rejected 2026-09-07). It is
  the one file a clinician might be handed directly, but a fourth copy of the intended-purpose
  statement is a fourth thing to keep in sync, and the website's is the one a clinician is sent.
- **Generate the website from this repository** (rejected 2026-09-07): it would put a public build
  pipeline in the tree that holds the operating contract and bring back tracked generated bytes,
  with a live egress surface attached.
- **`CLAUDE.md` naming the website** (the rule from 2026-09-07; reversed by the keystone split,
  2026-09-08): `CLAUDE.md` became the care contract; agents orient on it, humans on the website.

## Consequences

- One human surface instead of two, maintained where it is read.
- The repository holds no tracked generated HTML, a drift liability ADR-0043 accepted knowingly and
  paid for with a guard.
- `.venv-render/` is needed only to render and seal handouts (ADR-0039), not by the guard board.
- The export shrinks by the site pages, the builder and `OVERVIEW.md`.
- The site's residual moves rather than closes: with L17's handle lane gone, the flip-time export
  scan and the care-side relay scan remain. That is acceptable only because a development tree
  holds no record (ADR-0050).
- Verified by the board, the bite suite and a dry export without the three removed entries.
